What This Shows

From Gutenberg's first experiments in the 1450s to the close of the 17th century, European printing underwent exponential growth. The curve is not linear - it accelerates dramatically after 1500, with particularly strong growth during the Reformation (1520s) and again during the Scientific Revolution and religious conflicts of the 17th century.

By 1700, over 1.6 million editions had been printed - an average of nearly 18,000 new titles per year in the final decade. This represents one of the most significant information revolutions in human history.

Source: Universal Short Title Catalogue, University of St Andrews. ustc.ac.uk
